Where AI advertising fits

ChatGPT Ads is a distinct paid channel. AI-assisted creative, automated bidding on established ad platforms and organic visibility in AI assistants are separate disciplines. I assess each against your business goals rather than assuming every AI product sells advertising inventory.

Frequently asked questions

Can I advertise on ChatGPT in Dubai?

Yes. OpenAI currently lists UAE-based billing entities as eligible for self-service Ads Manager. Individual account requirements and campaign review apply.

Can I work directly with Niyaz Yazwan for ChatGPT Ads strategy?

Yes. Niyaz the MRKTR offers independent planning, readiness reviews and measurement consulting. View his professional profile.

Where do ChatGPT ads appear?

Sponsored placements may appear separately below responses for eligible users. They do not change the answers.

How much does ChatGPT advertising cost?

Costs depend on available buying options, competition and campaign settings. Review current account requirements and agree on a controlled test budget.

Can ChatGPT Ads generate leads?

A relevant offer and landing page can support lead acquisition. Measure actual qualified enquiries before drawing conclusions about performance.

Can advertisers see private ChatGPT conversations?

No. Advertisers do not receive private chat histories. Use aggregate platform reporting and consented first-party measurement.

Can I advertise on Claude or Gemini too?

Advertising access differs by product and market. I assess officially available inventory rather than assuming every AI assistant accepts paid placements.

How is ChatGPT Ads different from GEO?

Ads are paid placements. GEO focuses on unpaid visibility in generated answers. Advertising does not influence ChatGPT answers.

How do you measure AI advertising?

Use available ad reporting, campaign-tagged landing pages, GA4 and CRM outcomes. Compare qualified acquisition costs and avoid double counting.
