THE PERSON BEHIND MRKTR

Marketing is in my veins.

Marketing has been in my veins since childhood. I found myself watching commercials from Ogilvy and admiring the storytelling of Piyush Pandey and Prakash Varma. It wasn't just the product or the message that stayed with me—it was how one clever idea could make someone feel something and remember it.

After graduation, I left home for Bengaluru to explore that curiosity for real. The journey eventually led me to Dubai, where I've worked across growth, performance advertising, education, culture and creative campaigns. 12 years of experience later, I'm still exploring—AI, photography, technology and every new way to make ideas visible and results measurable.

Beyond marketing, I am drawn to the quieter art of observing life. Photography is my way of preserving emotions that words often miss. With my Nikon and a love for analogue photography, film grain and imperfect frames, I find beauty in the unscripted. Technology shapes how I work, but emotion shapes how I see.
